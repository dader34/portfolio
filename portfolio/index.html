About Me

I'm a 20 year old developer from Washington with a passion for building things that work well and look good doing it. I got into coding as a kid by tearing apart Roblox game scripts, figuring out how they worked and finding ways to break them. That curiosity turned into a genuine love for code.

These days I build full-stack web apps, developer tools, and everything in between. I still love finding exploits and poking at systems to see how they tick. Cars, driving, and all things technology round out the rest of my time. I'm currently studying cybersecurity at Everett Community College.
